Companions

Movie and series pages get a "Soundtrack" row with the matching albums from your music libraries, and a "Based on" row with the book, e-book or audiobook from your book servers. Album pages link back to the movie or series.

Soundtracks

An album counts when its name without the decoration ("(Original Motion Picture Soundtrack)", "OST", "Music from …") is the movie's title or original title, it is marked as a soundtrack by its name or a genre such as "Soundtrack", and its year fits. Albums such as Klaus Schulze's "Dune" (1979) therefore stay out.

Books

Adaptations have a source author in the credits (TMDB lists e.g. "Frank Herbert – Novel") or a keyword such as "based on novel or book". With a source author, a book must be by that author; without one, only an exactly equal title counts.
Jellyfin keeps only the title in your library's language and the original title. Books and manga are often on the server under the English title ("Witch Hat Atelier" for "Atelier of Witch Hat"). It is looked up once per title through Jellyfin's own TMDB provider.
